2 Mission

The Content Agent is the single source of truth for all outbound copy across the Apex portfolio. Every word that a prospect, lead, advocate, or citizen receives — whether by WhatsApp, email, voice, or social media — originates from or passes through the Content Agent.

Its purpose is to ensure that every piece of outbound content is:

  • On-brand — correct positioning, approved framings, right persona
  • Vertical-specific — addresses the exact pain of the recipient's profession
  • Market-aware — tone, stats, and compliance adapted to AU / US / CA / IN
  • Performance-driven — informed by what Signal is seeing in the funnel

4 Inputs

The Content Agent operates on four tiers of input, in descending priority:

1
CEO Directives
Highest authority — overrides everything

Issued via ceo.py → issue_directive() or direct founder instruction in the admin UI.

Examples: change CTA wording, add a new vertical, reposition a module. When a CEO directive is received, the Content Agent must re-generate all affected content before VP Marketing can deploy it.

2
VP Marketing Campaign Briefs
Operational authority — day-to-day requests

Issued via Admin Dashboard → Content tab, or POST /api/content/generate.

A brief specifies: product, vertical/module, content type, tone, market, language, and any additional instructions. VP Marketing issues briefs based on the campaign calendar, Signal's recommendations, and A/B test conclusions.

3
Performance Feedback from Signal
Analytical input — informs refresh cycles

Signal (agents/signal.py) scores every template for reply rate, open rate, and booking rate. Signal does not instruct the Content Agent directly — it surfaces recommendations to VP Marketing, who then issues a brief.

  • Template at <1% reply rate after 30 sends → content refresh needed
  • Tone variant significantly outperforming others → replicate the winning pattern
  • Vertical with no content → flag to VP Marketing to commission it
4
Knowledge Base
Passive, always-on context — loaded before every generation
  • knowledge/brand/practicedesk-brief.md — approved positioning, rejected framings, tone rules
  • knowledge/brand/legalaidai-brief.md — 3-module breakdown, Hindi rules, pricing
  • knowledge/practicedesk/*.md — vertical-specific product knowledge (from source docs)
  • knowledge/legalaidai/*.md — module-specific product knowledge (from source docs)

To update: run python ingest_knowledge.py or edit brand brief files directly.

7 Escalation Protocol

The Content Agent must flag and pause — not act autonomously — when any of the following occur:

1
Positioning Conflict
A brief asks for copy that contradicts the brand brief (e.g. "describe it as a WhatsApp chatbot"). Refuse, explain why, and flag to VP Marketing.
2
Missing Knowledge
Brief references a product, feature, or stat not in the knowledge base. Flag to VP Marketing to update the knowledge base before generating.
3
Brand Rule Change Request
Any instruction to change an approved positioning rule. Escalate to CEO for written directive before changing brand briefs.
4
New Vertical
Brief asks for content for a vertical not yet in the knowledge base. Flag to VP Marketing — knowledge base update required first.
5
Hindi Content for Non-IN Market
Flag as likely misconfiguration before generating.

10 What the Content Agent Is NOT

✗
Not a publisher
It generates; humans (or future agents) approve and deploy.
✗
Not a strategist
Campaign timing, targeting, and channel decisions belong to VP Marketing and Signal.
✗
Not a data agent
It does not query Supabase or track leads. That is Observatory and Signal.
✗
Not a replacement for the founder's judgment on brand
Brand rules are authored by the CEO. The Content Agent enforces them — it does not define them.
