Key Cost Assumptions

  • Voice Vapi billing: ~$0.12/min all-in (platform 0.05 + STT 0.03 + TTS 0.02 + telephony 0.02)
  • Estimated call volume: Starter ~50 min/mo · Growth ~100 min/mo · Pro ~200 min/mo
  • Claude Haiku used for WA/email; Sonnet for complex flows
  • WA: 100 / 200 / 400 conversations per plan per month (avg)
  • Infra is shared and mostly fixed — apportioned equally per active client

Scale Dynamics

  • Infrastructure cost per client drops sharply after 10 clients (fixed Railway cost spreads)
  • Claude API stays predictable — Haiku is very cheap per message
  • Vapi is the biggest variable cost; optimize by reducing avg call length (Sophie trained to ~3.5 min)
  • At 50 Pro clients: ~$6,700/mo gross profit from Pro alone
  • Setup fee ($180 USD / CAD $250) when charged covers ~15 months of infra cost per client

Competitive Positioning — PracticeDesk vs Weave

⚠️
Context: Not a Direct Competitor

Weave is a full practice management platform (phone system + reviews + payments + scheduling) used by established dental/medical practices in the US. It's hardware-dependent, requires IT setup, and targets practices with 3+ staff. PracticeDesk targets smaller practices (1–3 practitioners) who want AI-first, lightweight, fast-to-deploy.
