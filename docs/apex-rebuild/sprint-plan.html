4 · The message that wins

Against dental incumbents, lead with the wedge:

"Everything Weave does at your front desk — across phone, WhatsApp and email —
 for a fraction of the price, live in 15 minutes, no contract."

True, specific, and aimed at incumbents' weak points: price, setup friction, and opaque sales-call pricing. For Canada, lead dentists with the Canada Dental Care Plan angle (new patients calling now — capture them or lose them to voicemail).

5 · After the week

Let Signal show which subject/hook/channel converts. Double down on the winner, turn trial starts into paid with onboarding nudges + the demo flow, and only then clone the exact playbook to a second vertical. Before scaling sends further, move cold outreach to a warmed subdomain with SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
