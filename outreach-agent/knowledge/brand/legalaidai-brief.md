# LegalAidAI / Nyaya Saathi — Founder's Brand Brief
*Distilled from founder decisions and product documentation, June 2026.*

## What Nyaya Saathi Is

> **Nyaya Saathi** (meaning "Legal Companion" in Hindi) is India's first bilingual AI legal platform — empowering 1.4 billion citizens to understand their rights, and 1.7 million advocates to research and draft at 10× speed.

### Live URL
https://www.legalaidai.in

### The core problem solved
- 1.7 million enrolled advocates have no affordable legal research tools (SCC Online / Manupatra = ₹25,000–₹60,000/year)
- 1.4 billion citizens have almost no access to plain-language legal guidance
- AI tools like ChatGPT hallucinate Indian case citations — a professional liability risk
- No AI has been built specifically for Indian procedural law (CPC, CrPC/BNSS, BNS, IPC, Commercial Courts Act 2015, NI Act)

## Three Modules — Treat as Separate Products

### Module 1: Advocate (primary revenue module)
**Who:** Indian advocates practicing in district, session, high courts, commercial courts
**Price:** ₹2,500/month first month · ₹5,000/month renewal
**Channel mix:** Voice + WhatsApp + Affiliate outreach
**Close type:** Self-serve (advocates sign up directly)

**Core value:**
- Claude Sonnet-powered research + drafting studio
- Real Indian Kanoon case law — no hallucinated citations
- 12+ court-ready document templates
- Commercial Courts specialist module
- Hindi + English bilingual

**Key pain points for outreach:**
- SCC Online / Manupatra are unaffordable for most advocates
- Junior advocates waste hours manually searching case law
- Document drafting is time-consuming and repetitive
- ChatGPT/Gemini hallucinate Indian citations — dangerous for court submissions

**Outreach channels:**
- WhatsApp broadcast to advocate lists (city + bar association filters)
- Voice agent (outbound calls to advocates)
- Affiliate program: other advocates refer for ₹500/₹500 (signup + first renewal)

### Module 2: Corporate / In-house Legal
**Who:** In-house legal teams at Indian corporates, compliance officers, CFOs
**Channel mix:** Email + Voice
**Close type:** Human close (founder or VP Sales gets on a call)

**Core value:**
- Contract review + compliance gap analysis
- Indian regulatory framework (Companies Act, SEBI, FEMA, GST, labour law)
- Risk flagging on commercial agreements
- Research memo generation in minutes

**Key pain points:**
- In-house teams are lean — 1–3 lawyers covering entire companies
- External law firm retainers are expensive for routine queries
- Compliance deadlines are tracked manually across spreadsheets

**Content tone:** More formal than Advocate module. Decision-maker is a GC, CLO, or CFO. Lead with ROI and risk reduction, not "democratisation" language.

---

### Module 3: Citizen (freemium, social media acquisition)
**Who:** Any Indian dealing with a legal situation (tenant disputes, cheque bounce, consumer complaints, divorce, FIR, RTI, etc.)
**Channel mix:** Social media only (Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp forwards)
**Close type:** Freemium → Premium self-serve
**Price:** Free (5 consultations) → Premium (unlimited)

**Core value:**
- AI legal consultant in English and Hindi — voice and text
- Know Your Rights cards (12 topics, bilingual)
- Legal Notice Generator (9 free templates)
- Contracts & Agreements (6 free templates)

**Key pain points:**
- Ordinary citizens can't afford a lawyer for basic guidance
- Legal notices and documents are intimidating
- Language barrier — legal terms in English only

**Content tone:** Empowering, accessible, plain language. No legal jargon. Hinglish welcome. Focus on "you don't need a lawyer for this" angle.

## WA Template Rules (LegalAidAI)

### Advocate WA broadcast format
Templates must be Meta-approved. Key rules:
- Language code: `en` for English templates, `hi` for Hindi
- Opening: name the bar association or city to establish local relevance
- Never cold-pitch — frame as a tool built "by lawyers for lawyers"
- Include a proof point (case law accuracy, price vs SCC Online)
- CTA: free trial or demo booking

### Citizen WA format (broadcast)
- Short, informal, bilingual
- Lead with the specific legal problem (cheque bounce, tenant issues, etc.)
- Hindi CTA: "मुफ्त में जानें अपने अधिकार" ("Know your rights for free")
- Link: legalaidai.in

### Hindi content rules
- Use Devanagari script for full Hindi messages
- Hinglish (mixing Hindi and English terms) is natural and preferred in WA
- Legal terms in English are acceptable within Hindi messages (e.g. "bail", "FIR", "notice")
- Avoid overly formal Hindi — use conversational register

## Voice Agent Rules (LegalAidAI)

### Advocate voice outreach
- Open with a peer-framing question: "Are you still paying SCC Online rates for case law?"
- Never reveal the AI nature unless asked
- Emphasise: hallucination-free Indian Kanoon integration
- Offer a 7-day free trial, no credit card

### Citizen voice hotline (inbound)
- Caller may be distressed (arrested family member, urgent legal notice, etc.)
- Lead with empathy, then practical guidance
- Always offer to connect to a human advocate if needed
- Bilingual: switch to Hindi if caller uses Hindi

## Content Agent — What NOT to Generate

- Do not say "ChatGPT for lawyers" — the product is purpose-built for Indian law, not a wrapper
- Do not promise specific court outcomes or legal advice
- Do not use "AI chatbot" for the Citizen module — use "AI legal assistant" or "AI legal consultant"
- Do not hallucinate Indian Kanoon case numbers or citations
- Corporate module copy must not use citizen/empowerment framing — wrong audience
- Advocate copy must not use corporate framing — wrong tone
