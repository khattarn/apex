# LegalAidAI — Free Cause List Email Service

## Feature Overview

The **Free Cause List Email Service** is a zero-friction lead generation tool for the Advocate module.
Any practising advocate can sign up with their case CNR numbers and receive a daily morning email
showing which of their cases are listed in court that day, fetched live from eCourts/NJDG.

- **Free forever** — no login, no payment, no app required
- **Delivers genuine daily value** before the advocate pays anything
- **Every email footer** contains a CTA to start a 7-day free trial of NSP Pro

## The Pain Point

Every morning, advocates open eCourts and check each case number one by one to find out if it is
listed that day. With 20 active cases this takes 20 minutes — and they can still miss a hearing.

This service aggregates all their cases and delivers the answer in one email by 7:30 AM IST.

---

## Target Audience

**Primary:** Practising advocates in India — junior to mid-level — managing 10–40 active cases
across district courts and high courts. Currently checking eCourts manually every morning.

**Secondary:** Senior advocates with a large case load who want a single-glance morning briefing
without delegating to a clerk.

---

```
Advocate signs up free (email + CNR numbers)
  → Daily cause list email by 7:30 AM IST
  → Email footer CTA: "Start 7-day free trial →"
  → Day-14 nurture email
  → NSP Pro paid subscriber (₹2,500/month)
```

---

## What the Email Contains

- **Gold cards** — today's hearings (case name, court, time, stage)
- **Upcoming hearings** — this week's listings across all tracked cases
- **Count of cases with no hearing this week** — so they know what to ignore
- **Footer CTA** — "Start your 7-day free trial of NSP Pro →" with link to legalaidai.in/advocate

---

## Brand Voice for This Feature

- **Tone:** Trustworthy and peer-to-peer. Not salesy. Concise, credible, no hype.
- **Framing:** A practical tool built for how advocates actually work — not a product pitch.
- **Zero-friction emphasis:** "No login. No payment. Just your email address and CNR numbers."
- **Fear angle (use sparingly):** Missing a listed hearing is a real professional risk. Acknowledge it once; don't dwell.
- **Time-saving angle (primary):** 20 minutes every morning → one email at 7:30 AM.

## Channel-Specific Guidance

### WhatsApp forward message
- Short, bullet-friendly, designed to be forwarded in bar association groups
- Open with the pain (checking eCourts one case at a time)
- State the offer in one sentence — free, no app, no login
- End with the signup URL
- Two variants: short (3 lines) and longer (with bullets)
- Avoid exclamation marks; do not use emojis unless specifically requested

### LinkedIn post
- Story arc: relatable advocate morning → pain → reveal the product as a natural conclusion
- 200–250 words
- Professional tone — this audience is junior to mid advocates and legal tech followers
- End with the signup URL, no hashtag spam

### Bar Association outreach email
- Formal one-pager addressed to the Bar Association Secretary
- Frame as a service to members, not a sales pitch
- Ask them to circulate to members
- No pricing, no upsell — pure utility framing
- Sign-off from founder

### Email to existing LegalAidAI contacts (warm list)
- Announcement tone: "We've built something free for you"
- Personal, direct, link prominent
- Subject line must lead with the benefit, not the feature name
- One clear CTA, no secondary links

### Instagram carousel
- Title slide: "5 minutes you lose every morning (and how to get them back)"
- 5–6 slides walking through the problem and the solution
- Visual: phone showing eCourts (problem) → email in inbox (solution)
- Final slide: signup URL + "Free. No app. No login."

---

## What NOT to Generate for This Feature

- Do not position this as a product requiring payment — it is permanently free
- Do not promise that advocates will never miss hearings — say "reduces the risk of missing"
- Do not refer to it as an "AI tool" — the cause list fetch is from eCourts, not AI-generated
- Do not use "app" — there is no app; it is an email service
- Do not mention NSP Pro pricing in the signup-focused content (cause list CTA copy only)
- Do not use "revolutionary", "game-changing", or similar hype words
- The upsell is in the email footer, not the acquisition content — keep acquisition copy clean
