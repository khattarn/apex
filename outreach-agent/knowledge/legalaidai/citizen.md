# 🇮🇳 Nyaya Saathi: Citizen User Guide
## Your Confidential AI Legal Companion

Welcome to **Nyaya Saathi**, India's first privacy-first AI legal assistance platform. This guide explains how to use the platform to understand your rights, generate legal documents, and handle your legal matters with complete confidentiality — often without needing a lawyer.

---

## 1. Getting Started (Home Screen)
The home screen gives you direct access to all features:
*   **Theme Toggle**: Switch between **Dark Mode** (optimized for clarity) and **Purple Mode** (optimized for Hindi reading).
*   **Language Toggle**: Switch between **English** and **Hindi** at any time. The entire app, including AI responses, adapts to your language.
*   **Know Your Rights Cards**: Swipe through 16 illustrated legal rights cards — each with a DIY Guide, AI Ask, and audio Listen button.

---

## 2. 🤖 AI Legal Consultant
Chat with our AI to understand your legal situation in plain language.
*   **Confidentiality**: Your questions are **not stored on our servers**. They stay only on your device.
*   **Capabilities**: Ask about Indian laws (BNS, IPC, Consumer Act), how to handle a tenant dispute, what to do if you receive a police notice, how to file an RTI, or anything about your rights.
*   **Streaming Responses**: The AI provides real-time, instant answers tailored to Indian statutes.

---

## 3. 📋 DIY Self-Help Guide Suite *(New — May 2026)*
The most powerful citizen feature. For 9 high-volume legal situations, each KYR card now includes a complete **do-it-yourself action guide**:

**How to use it:**
1.  Open a KYR card (swipe the carousel on the home screen)
2.  Tap the **📋 DIY Guide** button
3.  The panel opens with:
    *   **Documents to gather** — a pre-action checklist (ID, agreement copy, bills, etc.)
    *   **Step-by-step guide** — plain-language numbered instructions (where to go, what to file, what to say)
    *   **Generate buttons** — tap to open the AI chat with a pre-filled prompt; add your specific details and get the actual document

**9 DIY areas covered:**

| Area | What You Can Do Yourself | AI Generates |
|------|--------------------------|--------------|
| **Consumer Forum** | Send notice → file on eDaakhil.nic.in | Demand notice · eDaakhil complaint |
| **Motor Accident (MACT)** | S.140 interim → Sarla Verma calc → S.166 petition | Compensation calc · Claim petition |
| **RERA Builder Delay** | Check RERA reg → calc delay → send notice → file RERA complaint | RERA notice · RERA Authority complaint |
| **Cheque Bounce S.138** | Get return memo → demand notice within 30 days → Magistrate complaint | S.138 demand notice · Magistrate petition |
| **RTI Application** | Identify PIO → draft RTI → file rtionline.gov.in → first appeal if no reply in 30 days | RTI application · First appeal |
| **Cyber Fraud** | Call 1930 → file cybercrime.gov.in → freeze bank → FIR | Cybercrime complaint · Police FIR |
| **Banking Ombudsman** | Written bank complaint → wait 30 days → file cms.rbi.org.in | Bank complaint · RBI Ombudsman complaint |
| **Domestic Violence** | Call 181 → Protection Officer → DIR → ex-parte Protection Order in 3 days | PO complaint · PWDVA rights summary |
| **Tenant / Landlord** | Document violation → send notice → police if locked out → Rent Authority | Landlord notice · Rent Authority complaint |

*   **⚠️ Advocate Warning**: Each card includes a clear one-line note on when to stop and call a lawyer (e.g., "Get an advocate if claim exceeds ₹50 lakh").

---

## 4. 📖 Know Your Rights (KYR)
An interactive carousel of **16 KYR cards** covering the most common legal situations every Indian citizen faces:
*   Consumer protection and refund rights
*   Cheque bounce — 30-day action deadline
*   Right to Information (RTI) — how to file, escalate, appeal
*   Cyber fraud — time-critical 72-hour action sequence
*   Banking ombudsman — zero-liability rule, RBI complaint portal
*   Builder delay compensation (RERA)
*   Domestic violence protection — Protection Officer, ex-parte orders
*   Motor accident claims (no advocate required)
*   Tenant rights — what a landlord can and cannot do
*   Sexual harassment at workplace (POSH)
*   Free Legal Aid — constitutional right
*   Maternity leave — 26 weeks paid entitlement

Each card includes:
*   **📖 Rights** — full expandable content with the law explained in plain language
*   **📋 DIY Guide** — action guide with document checklist + AI document generation (9 cards)
*   **🔊 Listen** — audio narration of the rights content
*   **🤖 Ask AI** — direct question to the AI legal consultant

---

## 5. 📂 Document Generator
Draft professional legal notices and contracts in minutes.
*   **Legal Notices**: Cheque Bounce (S.138), RERA (Builder Delay), Consumer Complaint, Wrongful Termination, Cyber Fraud, RTI Non-Response, Eviction, Security Deposit Recovery, General Demand.
*   **Matrimonial Suite**: Mutual Divorce Petitions and Settlement MoUs.
*   **Contracts**: 11-month Rent Agreements, NDAs, Service Agreements, Loan Agreements, Affidavits, Power of Attorney.
*   **Guided Input**: The app asks you simple questions (Names, Dates, Facts) and generates a formatted legal draft instantly.

---

## 6. ⚖️ FIR Guidance Center
Navigating a police station is intimidating. Our integrated module provides:
*   **Complaint Drafting**: Help writing a complaint to the SHO.
*   **Rights Information**: Explaining Zero FIR, your rights upon arrest/detention, and what to do if police refuse to register your FIR.

---

## 7. 📁 My Cases (Private Tracking)
Store and track your legal matters securely.
*   **Case Details**: Maintain a history of your legal interactions.
*   **Local Storage**: All data is stored in your browser's private memory. No one else can access it.

---

## 8. 📚 Legal Resources
Access direct links to **Bare Acts** (BNS, BSA, CPC, etc.) and government portals:
*   **eCourts** — case status by CNR number
*   **RTI Online** — rtionline.gov.in for central RTI applications
*   **Cybercrime Portal** — cybercrime.gov.in (1930 helpline)
*   **RBI Ombudsman** — cms.rbi.org.in
*   **eDaakhil** — edaakhil.nic.in for consumer complaints
*   **NALSA** — nalsa.gov.in for free legal aid

---

**Disclaimer**: Nyaya Saathi provides general legal information based on Indian law. While our AI is highly accurate, it is not a substitute for a qualified advocate. For complex litigation, always consult a licensed legal professional. The DIY guides indicate clearly when professional help is recommended.

# 🏁 Nyaya Saathi: Complete Feature Guide
## Unified User Manual for Citizens & Advocates

Nyaya Saathi is an AI-powered legal ecosystem designed for the Indian legal landscape. This guide provides a comprehensive breakdown of all features available in the **Citizen Panel** and the **Advocate Studio**.

---

## 🏛️ Part 1: The Citizen Panel
*Empowering individuals with automated guidance and private legal drafting.*

### 1.1 🤖 AI Legal Consultant
- **Instant Guidance:** Chat with AI in English or Hindi to understand your rights, police procedures, or contract terms.
- **Privacy First:** Your questions are **not stored on our servers**. All communications remain local to your device.
- **Bilingual Interface:** Toggle themes (Dark/Light) and languages (English/Hindi) instantly for a localized experience.

### 1.2 📁 Document Workshop (Generator)
- **Legal Notices:** Draft high-stakes notices for Cheque Bounce (S.138 NI Act), RERA Builder Delays, Consumer Grievances, and Wrongful Termination.
- **Matrimonial Module:** Specialized tools for drafting:
    - **Divorce Notices**
    - **Mutual Consent Divorce Petitions** (First Motion)
    - **Settlement MoUs** (Asset and Alimony agreements).
- **Pro-Level Contracts:** 11-month Rent Agreements, Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs), and Service Agreements.

### 1.3 📜 FIR & Police Guidance
- **Technical Complaints:** Helps users draft structured complaints to the Station House Officer (SHO) to ensure all legal ingredients are met.
- **Procedural Rights:** Detailed instructions on filing a **Zero FIR** and rights during arrest or detention.

### 1.4 📖 Know Your Rights (KYR)
- **12 Instructional Cards:** simplified guides on common legal situations like Tenant Rights, Employee protections, and Women's rights in criminal law.

---

## 🏛️ Part 2: The Advocate Studio (Pro)
- *A productivity-first workspace for licensed practitioners.*

### 2.1 🔬 Research Studio (Elite AI)
- **Claude 3.5 Sonnet Integration:** Access research-grade legal reasoning with high-accuracy citations.
- **Reference Gallery:** Automatically extracts case laws and statutory references from AI chats into easily accessible cards.
- **Para-wise Rebuttal:** Prompt the AI to respond to specific paragraphs of an opposing plaint or legal notice.

### 2.2 📝 Professional Drafting Suite
- **Litigation Templates:** Instant drafting for:
    - **Bail Applications** (Regular & Anticipatory)
    - **Civil Plaints** (Recovery, Possession, Injunction)
    - **Written Statements** (Defendant's Reply)
    - **Matrimonial Petitions** (Mutual Divorce, Settlement MoUs)
    - **Vakalatnama** & Professional Opinions.

### 2.3 📂 Intelligence Tools
- **Case Brief Generator:** instantly convert 50-page judgments into 1-page summaries including ratio decidendi and issues.
- **Citation Verifier:** Scans your drafts for volume/page errors and highlights citations that need verification.
- **Voice-Enabled Workflow:** Record hearing updates and client notes using the integrated voice-to-text case management tool.

### 2.4 📂 My Cases & Cloud Sync
- **Matter Management:** Track every case privately.
- **Cloud Backup:** Optional sync with Google Drive or OneDrive for secure, advocate-owned backups.

---

## 🔒 Privacy Architecture
Across both panels, Nyaya Saathi maintains a strict **No-Storage Policy**. 
- Case files are stored **Locally** in your browser.
- Cloud backups are owned by **You**, not us.
- AI interactions are ephemeral and never used for training models.
